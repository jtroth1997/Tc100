Before requesting quotes

Give tilers the information they need.

A clear enquiry helps a tiler understand the job and makes quotations easier to compare.

  • PhotographsInclude the whole space and any problem areas.
  • Approximate measurementsRoom size, wall areas and tile dimensions are useful.
  • Your chosen tilesShare the material, format, pattern and whether they are already purchased.
  • Timescale and accessExplain when the room is available and any parking or access limits.
  • Existing surfacesTell them what is currently fitted and who will remove it.

Questions worth asking every tiler.

You do not need to be a tiling expert. These questions make responsibilities and expectations clearer before work starts.

01

Have you completed similar work that I can see?

02

What preparation and waterproofing are included?

03

Who supplies adhesive, grout, trims and other materials?

04

Is waste removal included in the quotation?

05

What insurance do you hold for this type of work?

06

What could change the price or completion date?

Compare like for like

A useful quote describes the job—not just a total.

Look for preparation, waterproofing where needed, fixing materials, setting out, trims, grout, sealant, waste, exclusions, payment stages and an estimated timescale.

Good decisions protect the project

Take your time before saying yes.

Do not feel pressured to appoint someone immediately. Check recent references, confirm who is responsible for each part of the work and keep the agreed scope in writing.

Remember

The cheapest quotation is not automatically the best value. Missing preparation or unclear responsibilities can cost more later.
