Example system

JACKOBOARD Plano

  1. Choose the right board and thicknessWall, floor and substrate conditions change the fixing method.
  2. Fix or bed it correctlyWashers, screws, adhesive or a full bed may be required depending on the background.
  3. Treat joints and penetrationsWet-area performance depends on the specified joint, corner and fixing details.
  4. Use the right tile adhesiveBoard installation and tile fixing can have different adhesive requirements.
  5. Check the actual manufacturer documentThe guide points to current wall, floor, technical and approval literature.

What’s it used for?

A lightweight, tile-ready construction board for suitable wall and floor applications, bathroom construction and formed details. The XPS core is water-repellent and thermally insulating.
